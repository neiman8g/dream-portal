Product master

Layers that build the part number for us

Product group, then product, then the UL-listed capacity and voltages. Picking those builds the unit part number automatically, in each company's own prefix. Batteries and options hang off the product and carry their own part numbers.

Groups, products and capacities

  • Product groups such as Emergency Lighting Inverter UL924, Elevator Backup, Fire Alarm Backup UL1481.
  • Each product has a name and prefix for each company (Power Wave / Wave Rider / Powerride).
  • Capacities and voltages each carry the code they add to the part number.
  • Tick the combinations that are UL listed and the units are created for you.

Batteries and options

  • Batteries are matched to capacity, by runtime and type, with their own part numbers per company.
  • Options are shared by every capacity of a product: option group, then category, then sub-option, then an assigned part number.
  • That assigned part number carries the price and description for each company.
  • 15 option groups: breakers, bypass, communication, transfer, battery, short-circuit rating, cabinet, TVSS, LCR, services, warranty, spares, field service, delta input, frequency.
Pricing

Three price books, one item master

Every part number has a price for OLP, CPP and PPS. Online Power sells to the other two at a transfer price we control. OEMs and private labels buy at a multiplier we set per product.

Item master and price tracking

  • Every part, grouped by type: units, batteries, options, breakers, services.
  • Price, part number and description written three ways, one per company.
  • Component cost on every item, so margin shows everywhere. It will come from MRP later.
  • Every price change is recorded automatically: old price, new price, date and reason.
  • Variance report flags items where company prices drift apart.
  • Mass update: raise a product, a group or everything by a percent, with rounding.

Transfer price and private labels

  • Transfer price = OLP sell price × a percent per company (69% today), adjustable per item.
  • Private-label and OEM customers get a default multiplier plus a multiplier for each product (Wall Mount, Power Wave 1 and so on).
  • A quote for a private label is priced at base price × that product's multiplier.
CRM

Companies by territory, people by company

Our reps, distributors, engineers, contractors and OEMs are organized by territory number and company type, color-coded by how the account is doing.

What it does

  • Filter by territory, company type and status (engineer spec, sales progress, late, no activity, non-performing rep).
  • Each company lists its people with title, email and phone.
  • Notes on the whole company or on one person: calls, emails, meetings, visits.
  • The company page shows its quotes and starts a new one.
Quoting

Quotes built from the master, never typed in

Start from a company, pick which of our companies is quoting, then configure: capacity, voltages, transformer, battery, options. Each configuration lands on the quote as a section with every part number and price filled in.

What it does

  • Quote as OLP, CPP or PPS, each with its own part numbers and prices.
  • Private-label quotes apply that customer's multiplier per product.
  • Prices are locked when a line is added, so later price changes don't alter a sent quote. Reprice on purpose when needed.
  • Totals show sell, transfer from OLP, cost and margin.
Still to build

The rest of the flow

These are not in the prototype yet. They are where INMASS and our hand-built BOMs get replaced.

Sales orders

  • Won quote converts to a sales order with locked prices.
  • Quote PDFs in each company's branding, and quote revisions.

Engineering

  • Queue of new orders waiting for engineering.
  • Draft BOM generated from the order's units, batteries and options.
  • BOM revisions and approval, then release to MRP.

MRP and inventory

  • Multi-level BOMs, stock on hand, shortages.
  • Suggested purchase orders for components.
  • Component cost feeds back into the item master.

Manufacturing

  • Work orders and routings.
  • Component consumption and finished goods into stock.

Circuit breakers

  • Pick the right breaker from phase, capacity, voltage, input or output, and KAIC.

Access

  • Logins with roles: admin, sales, engineering, view-only.
  • Reps and distributors see only their own quotes.
For Odoo

What has to work, and what we need to know

Must work

  1. Smart part numbers built from capacity, input voltage, output voltage and transformer, in three company prefixes.
  2. One item with three part numbers, three descriptions and three prices (OLP, CPP, PPS).
  3. Inter-company sales from OLP to CPP and PPS at an adjustable transfer percent.
  4. Private-label multipliers per customer and per product.
  5. Full price history and variance between companies.
  6. Options shared across all capacities of a product, down to an assigned part number.
  7. Quote → sales order → BOM → MRP → manufacturing with no re-keying.
  8. Import of our current master and INMASS data.

Questions

  • How would you model one item sold under three companies with different part numbers? Multi-company with product variants, or separate products linked together?
  • Can the product configurator generate our part number format, including suffixes like -VA?
  • How do pricelists handle a multiplier per private-label customer per product?
  • How is the inter-company transfer price posted, and can it be a percent of the parent's sell price?
  • Can a won quote generate a draft BOM from its lines automatically?
  • What does migrating from INMASS look like, and how long?
